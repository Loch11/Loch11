Be Known.

A common problem in brand identity is "sameness." Businesses within an industry often look and feel the same, which makes it difficult for them to stand out. If you are looking to break the mold and emerge from the crowd, click the button below and let's talk about how to set your brand apart from the rest.

Let's Talk

Loch11 is a design studio in the New Hampshire Seacoast region. We specialize in creating illustrative brand assets that help businesses emerge from the sea of sameness. Our services include logos/branding, mascot design and illustration.

How we work

1

Discover

In the discovery phase we problem solve. We want to know what your vision for your business is. This is where we get to know each other and I learn about your business, your goals, and your target audience so we can decide what the areas are that need improvement and what strategies we can implement in your visual communications. During our initial meeting, we'll discuss the project scope, timeline, and expectations. We'll determine how many revisions are included, pricing, and how deliverables will be shared. For my process, I require a 50% non-refundable deposit to begin work, with the remaining 50% due upon completion and approval of the project. Deliverables will be shared after final payment is received.

2

Research

I'll dive deep into the competition and really get to know your industry inside and out. This phase is critical in the design process for making design decisions and finding the best solutions for your brand.

3

Design

Once I'm immersed in the market research, I'll use what I've learned about you, your brand, your target audience, and your competition to create brand assets that make your brand known to your audience and stand out from the competition.

4

Revisions

Based on your feedback, I'll make the necessary revisions to ensure the final deliverables align with your vision and meet your expectations. The number of revisions will be determined during onboarding and contract discussion.
